Hi there, and thanks for dropping by! Pull up a chair. I made this page because I am tired of watching people argue about whose body counts and whose years count while nobody asks who is making the rules. Laws get written about bodies every single day: who may do what with theirs, who gets looked at, who gets looked past. And somewhere along the way folks decided that being old (or being called "too young") means you can be ignored at the town meeting.

I don't think that is right, and I have a keyboard, a 28.8 modem and a free homepage, so here we are. This site is part history, part rant and part scrapbook. Disagree with me! I mean it. A good argument is the whole point of this neighborhood.

WHAT YOU WILL FIND HERE:

  • Body Politics. Who owns a body once the lawmakers walk in? Clippings, questions and my own two cents on bodies, rules and rights.
  • Ageism. Too old to hire, too young to be heard. Stories from my grandmother's kitchen table and my first job interview.
  • The Soapbox. My opinion of the week. It changes. Sometimes I am even wrong.
  • Write to Your Rep. A little how-to for sending a real letter to a real congressperson. Stamps still work!
  • Sign My Guestbook and tell me what you think.
  • Cool Links. Other homesteaders on Capitol Hill who share my passion for a good debate.
